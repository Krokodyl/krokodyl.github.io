Dragonlord

 August 17, 2026

Dragonlord is an Atari ST game developed by Thy & Jo and published by 16-32 Diffusion.


     

IPS Patch


Here is an IPS patch that fixes 5 bugs listed below.

v1.1: DragonLord - Fix 1.1.zip
v1.0: DragonLord - Fix 1.0.zip

Apply the IPS patch to the STX file:
Dragon Lord - 16-32 Diffusion.stx
File size: 1650272 ($192e60)
SHA1: 3A6BA776A8355EFA01EBCCE84BB889FEBAEA21D3
MD5: 8C5463751AD230AA34183B44C751205B
CRC32: A060188A

Bugs fixed v1.0:

  • Numbers above six spelled incorrectly
  • Transition screen between stage 3 and 4
  • Wrong palette in final area
  • Wrong palette on end screen
  • Words spelled incorrectly on end screen

Bugs fixed v1.1:

  • Jittery colors during intro

Bugs (Ending Spoiler)


01 - Misspelled numbers


Original version Fixed version

02 - Transition screen



The screen for the transition to and from stage 4 flashes for 1 sec and disappears. It’s due to an off-by-one error.


03 - Wrong palette in final area


Original version Fixed version

The final area uses the shop palette instead of the normal palette.


04 - Wrong palette on end screen + spelling


Original version Fixed version

The game loads the correct palette but then loads the palette & image for the “insert name” screen. When the end screen is loaded, it kept the wrong palette.
Maybe the “insert name” screen was an afterthought and inserted at the wrong place.

As for the spelling, there’s a minus one after the text is loaded.

Considering the wrong palette at the end, it’s fair to assume that it’s not intentional.


05 - Jitter during intro (1.1)


Original version Fixed version

That bug is due to the Timer B interrupt (handling scanline effects) not being allowed to stop Timer D interrupt (handling music).

Word from the dev


Someone reached out to the dev, who was sixteen at the time and his brother was eighteen, and he confirmed that these were not intentional:

SEVENA, AAAAA and so on are bugs, but I don’t remember them in our version (before 16/32 inserted its intro). The same for the last screen, supposed to be in red/orange and looking like a dawn on Grand Canyon. Again I do not know if it comes from your “disquette”, from your emulator, or from the production stage.

He said that the end of production was rushed and the game barely tested.
